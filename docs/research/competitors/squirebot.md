# The Squire Bot (thesquirebot.com) — competitive research

First pass 2026-11-16, deep pass 2026-11-17, recapture and full JS re-read 2026-11-28. All passes used the disposable trial dashboard account; its trial expired on 2026-11-18. On 2026-11-28 the real account still failed to log in ("Invalid credentials" for the stored email and password, and for the game username with the game password). No game account was added, and nothing was started, saved, bought or toggled. Every non-GET request except the login POST was blocked in the browser.

Screenshots, page text and an API/route table are in `.audit/squirebot/` (see its `README.md`). The 2026-11-28 pass is in `.audit/squirebot/r2/`: 39 route captures, every module's JS chunk with extracted strings (`js/`), and the per-module findings (`findings-A..D.md`) behind the corrections below.

Evidence column:

- **UI:** rendered in the dashboard and captured in `.audit/squirebot/`.
- **JS bundle:** these forms need a linked game account or a higher plan, so they never rendered. Their labels, option lists, defaults and help text were read from the site's public client JS chunks. They reflect what the UI offers, but nobody saw them rendered.
- **Marketing:** the landing page or Overview copy only.

## Summary

- **Product:** a hosted cloud bot. The tab title is "The Squire Bot" and the sidebar says "GGE Bot".
  - Next.js (Turbopack) with shadcn/Radix UI, a dark gold theme and 4 colour themes: Warm, Creme, Royal Green and Sharp.
  - You add game accounts (game username, game password, server). The bot logs in server-side and runs modules 24/7. Nothing is installed locally.
  - Data loads with REST GETs to `/api/*`. Each module stores one JSON config per account (`PUT /api/accounts/<acct>/config/<module>`). A WebSocket at `/ws` pushes stats, logs, notifications and account changes. Module pages also poll a `/status` endpoint every 3–15 s.
- **Games:** the Add Account server list has 39 Goodgame Empire servers and ~35 **Empire: Four Kingdoms** servers (`e4k_*`, labelled "E4K <name>"). E4K accounts load unit and tool lists from `/api/modules/game-options`, and Rift groups refuse to mix E4K and GGE accounts. The landing page mentions only Goodgame Empire.
- **Sign-up:** username, email and password, or Discord OAuth. There is no captcha. Email verification is needed only "when requesting an extension".
- **Pricing model (credits, sold manually through Discord):**
  - Every new account gets a **free 2-day trial of the Green plan**. Overview showed "TIME LEFT 1d 14h until Nov 18, 03:15 PM".
  - **Green plan: 12 creds.** It covers all main-realm modules on all your accounts.
  - **BTH add-on: +4 creds.** It covers the Beyond the Horizon modules.
  - **Outers:** a future separate tier. The landing page (2026-11-28) says "only MP Builder is open for use for the time being". The client already has OR Storms, a full Outers tab in Coin Farmer, and "Enable Outers" in BTH Blacksmith.
  - Licences card: "RUNNING 0 / 1 · 1 free · 0 saved". "One licence = one account running at once (main and event together). Saved accounts are not limited." Shared accounts start on their owner's licences. With no free licence, a "Swap a licence" dialog stops one account and starts the other with its saved set. During a deploy restore the licence is held for up to 30 min.
  - **Expired access** (seen 2026-11-28): "running modules keep running, but new starts are blocked, and if a connection drops it won't reconnect or restore". The tile shows "0 / 0", the BTH/Outers pages show the locked card, and Green pages still render and can be edited offline.
  - **Teams (new since 11-17):** the admin assigns team leaders, and "a leader adds you by your dashboard username". A leader can grant licences: "Your own licences first, then each leader's grant (members run on the leader's days and modules)". On the Team page (`/dashboard/team`, hidden from the sidebar unless you are in a team), a leader sees "Your team — licences" (total and handed out) and uses **"Hand out licences"**: a member's dashboard username plus a count ("0 = a member without licences"). If no dashboard account has that username yet, the grant waits ("it becomes theirs when they open the dashboard"). Grants can be edited or removed, and a member can be promoted with "Make manager" ("Managers may edit the accounts members share with the team") and demoted with "Member again". Members see "Your teams" with each leader, the licences running on that grant and its modules (all, or a list). The page also lists "Accounts shared with you": starts use the owner's saved quick-start set limited to your access, and "Cancel my modules" cancels only your part. It also lists "Accounts you have shared". Endpoints: `/api/team`, `/api/team/grants`, `/api/team/grants/<id>` (PUT, DELETE), `/api/team/grants/<id>/role`. In effect a leader buys licences and resells or shares them within a team.
  - Credit prices are not public. Locked modules show a full-page card: "Not included in your plan … Contact the admin (thesquire_2 or sekyra_) to add it." There is no self-serve checkout.
- **Trial scope:** Green only. Coin Farmer, BTH Blacksmith, Influence Farming, Equipment Manager, OR Storms and MP Builder were plan-locked. Event Recruiter and BTH Castle Builder showed their account picker.
- **Account sharing:** an owner shares a game account with a person (by dashboard username) or with their team's leader and managers. The choice is Full access, or Custom: a module checklist plus six capabilities (start and stop, module settings, commanders and presets, account settings, **quick-start sets**, Discord notifications). Reading is always allowed. A user with only the commanders right edits just a module's "commander choices". "I'm playing on this account" (hands-off) blocks starts by others and by bulk runs ("Owner is playing — starts are off."). Shared users can never change the password, remove the account or change sharing. "Your game password, saved Discord destination and proxy are always private." There is an editor activity log (last 10 changes).
- **Overview bulk tools:** groups of accounts (personal or team, 1–60-character names; "gives nobody access to anything"); filters (mine, shared, owner, status, module); bulk Start/Stop/Cancel/Disconnect, which starts each account's saved quick-start set "one account at a time, 0.5 s apart", with a dry-run list of skip reasons; per-account settings export to JSON ("never passwords or Discord links"); "Apply settings" from another account or a file ("Castles are matched by kingdom and kind, never by id … Commanders, attack presets and targets stay each account's own").
- **Quick start:** each account has a saved quick-start set (only modules with a saved config) and a separate event set for the current BTH/Outers realm. The start pipeline shows progress and has Cancel start. Quick-stop can also disconnect. Starts queued for after maintenance can be withdrawn.
- **Community:** Discord server "FarmTheEmpire" (invite d8QRyuAAj2). The site links no docs, FAQ, changelog or YouTube.

## Feature inventory

Free/Paid key:

- **Trial/Green:** available in the trial. In steady state it is the paid Green plan (12 creds), marked "paid – advertised, not verified".
- **BTH:** +4 creds, paid – advertised, not verified.
- **Outers:** future tier.

Shared building blocks used by many modules:

- **Waves dialog:**
  - Waves 1–4 are collapsible. Each wave picks an in-game Server Preset (Empty, or Preset 1–30).
  - Toggle between Manual Config and Virtual Preset.
  - 3 support-tool (AST) slots.
  - 8 courtyard (RW) unit + count rows.
  - "The 4th wave's config is repeated for every extra wave."
- **Autofill dialog:**
  - Fill sides: Left, Front, Right, Courtyard.
  - Waves: 1–4.
  - Tools (wave 1): the options depend on the module.
  - Unit order: Fastest, Strongest or Weakest first.
  - Troop supply: Food, Mead or All.
  - Min troops: a hard floor of one wave's base capacity always applies.
- **Per-module commander list:** semicolon-separated, e.g. "1;2;3". It is checked against the account's commander count and for duplicates.

## Notable for a competitor

- **Account sharing with capability rights** (six capabilities plus a module checklist), team shares, a "hands off while the owner plays" lock and an editor activity log. **Teams** let a leader grant licences and modules to members, a reseller or guild model. This is a real multi-user feature, not just multi-account.
- **Fleet tooling:** account groups, bulk start/stop paced 0.5 s apart with dry-run skip reasons, settings export/import and "apply settings" across accounts with castle matching by kingdom and kind.
- **E4K support** in the same dashboard (`e4k_*` servers).
- **Breadth of event and economy coverage:**
  - Shop automation with 4 launch rules and per-event caps.
  - Coin-overflow burn.
  - Khan rage optimiser with ordered rage and tablet pick lists.
  - Berimond Advisor Mode, which uses the game's own repeat attacks.
  - Lane goals and a camp builder.
  - Food Manager rescue logistics.
  - Overseer keep-active versus sale-only buying.
  - Equipment selling and forging with relic-bonus protections.
  - A castle manager with an isometric layout, castle items and deco storage.
- **Defensive PvP is deep:** automatic stationing to protected alliance castles, repeat 12-hour stationing, open-gate fallbacks, maintenance-time stationing, seasonal-raid handling, and Discord pings per account.
- **Safety by limits, not humanization:**
  - Ruby floors and daily caps everywhere.
  - Attack limits by hit count or by coin cost, with a preview calculator.
  - Loss limits in Berimond.
  - A refusal counter that stops modules and asks the user to contact the admin.
  - No delay, jitter, proxy or schedule settings.
- **Polish:**
  - Long, specific help text on every control.
  - Offline config editing.
  - Live status cards on every module.
  - Purchase and transfer records.
  - A Discord live stats card.
  - Logs in the user's time zone with a live tail.
- **Gaps:**
  - No humanization or time-window scheduler. A per-account proxy exists server-side but users can't set it.
  - No quests, daily rewards or gifts.
  - Notifications go only to Discord; there is no Telegram or email.
  - Credits are billed manually through Discord.
  - The Outers tier has not launched.
  - The trial excludes BTH.
